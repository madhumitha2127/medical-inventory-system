## 📌 Overview

The **Medical Inventory System** is designed to simplify and organize medical inventory operations.

It helps manage medical stock, monitor product expiry dates, handle billing activities and maintain inventory information in a structured way.

The system aims to improve inventory visibility, reduce manual work and support efficient healthcare stock management.

## 🎯 Key Features

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Maintain medical product records.
- Track available stock quantities.
- Organize inventory information.

### ⏳ Expiry Date Tracking
- Monitor product expiry dates.
- Identify products approaching expiry.
- Support better stock monitoring.

### 🧾 Billing Management
- Manage billing information.
- Maintain transaction records.
- Simplify billing operations.

### 🔲 QR Code Integration
- Support QR code-based product identification.
- Make product information easier to access.

### 📊 Inventory Analytics
- View inventory-related information.
- Support stock monitoring and management decisions.

## ⚙️ System Workflow

1. Add medical product details.
2. Store inventory information.
3. Track stock quantities.
4. Monitor expiry dates.
5. Process billing operations.
6. Use QR codes for product identification.
7. Review inventory information and analytics.
